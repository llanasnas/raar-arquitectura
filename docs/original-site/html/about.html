03 : About us

RAAR arquitectura

RAAR is born from the meeting of three passionate views, forged in Barcelona, united by a common intuition: architecture can be a transforming force, conscious and free, that finds its expression in timelessness. From Barcelona, ​​this project explores matter, light and space with the aim of creating projects that respect their environment.

We firmly believe in an architecture that is built with subtle gestures, with integration and also with the nobility of the materials that nature gives us. An architecture that does not need ostentation to have presence, that manifests itself in its essence and not in excess. Each project is an opportunity to rethink how we live, how we move and how we live with what surrounds us.

At RAAR, we understand creation as a constantly evolving process, where technique and intuition complement each other. We work with a firm commitment to a more responsible architecture, which responds to the real needs of people and the environment. Building is a long and complex process that invites us on every occasion to rethink how we relate to spaces.
